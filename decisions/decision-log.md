# Decision log

Append-only. Newest entry at the top. A decision is not a report. Record the choice, the reason, and how you will know it was wrong.

## Template

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD — <decision title>
- Status: Pursue | Watch | Pass | Revisit
- Related: (report and/or opportunity paths)
- Decision:
- Why:
- Revisit date:
- Kill criteria:
```
